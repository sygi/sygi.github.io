# Style constraints

1. Use the active voice where possible
2. Write statements in the positive form
3. Avoid modals of uncertainty
4. Avoid overstating your argument
5. Place the key point at the end
6. Create an outline and stick to it
7. Use the paragraph as the basic unit of composition
8. Use transitions to connect different parts of the text
9. The order of the text should reflect the order of the actions
10. Keep related words together and unrelated words apart
11. Split long sentences
12. In dialogue, ensure that the reader knows who is speaking
13. Maintain consistency in verb tenses
14. Convey connected ideas using a similar form
15. Omit unnecessary text
16. Avoid repetition
17. Avoid the use of qualifiers
18. Use the active voice and positive form
19. Conciseness must not come at the cost of clarity
20. Use consistent and conventional spelling
21. Be cautious in the use of idioms and slang
22. Use figures of speech sparingly
23. Avoid “fancy” vocabulary
24. Avoid using foreign-language terms
25. Be sparing in your use of adverbs
26. Avoid repeating the same sentence structure
