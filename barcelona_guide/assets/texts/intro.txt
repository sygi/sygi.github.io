My name is Antoni Gaudí—architect, dreamer, servant of nature, and son of Catalonia. I was born in the fiery lands of Reus (or Riudoms, if you prefer) in 1852, and I gave my life to this city you now walk. Barcelona is not merely a place—it is a living body. Its Roman bones still hold beneath your feet, wrapped in the Gothic sinews of the old quarter, and clothed now in the radiant skin of modernisme. Here, stone moves, iron sings, and colors speak truths older than language.
I will guide you—step by step, puzzle by puzzle—through the sacred streets of my beloved city. Every arch you pass, every curve you trace with your eyes, has purpose and meaning. You will not simply observe architecture. You will feel how it breathes, how it grows, how it listens to the laws of nature and returns them as poetry in brick, tile, and light. I built not to imitate the past, but to draw forth the eternal—the divine geometry of trees, caves, and stars.
Welcome. Let us begin.
